## 3. Tenant isolation & role-based permissions — IMPLEMENTED (config + code verified)

- `FinancialTx` and `FinancialAuditLog` RLS: create/read/update restricted to `owner_email == user.email` or admin; delete admin-only. Owner is stamped server-side by `financeIngest` from `resolveTenant()` — clients cannot choose their tenant.
- `resolveTenant`: admin → sdr_admin books; contractor → matched by Partner email; white-label → matched by WhiteLabelClient email; otherwise personal.
- `financeIngest update` whitelists editable fields only and audit-logs every correction with before/after detail.
- Runtime cross-tenant test still pending: needs two real (non-test) contractor accounts to confirm one cannot read the other's records through both the UI and Rex chat. **Recommended before onboarding real white-label clients.**
- Sandbox note: 5 ZZTEST partner records exist with `verified: true` — they are correctly excluded from capacity counts by `isTestPartner`, but consider setting `verified: false` on them to eliminate any accidental surface elsewhere.

## 4. Rex financial understanding — IMPLEMENTED (verified at config level)

- Rex resolves the signed-in user's business context first, then answers only from that tenant's records (income, expenses, tax jurisdictions per-transaction, missing receipts = empty `receipt_uri`, review flags). - Receipt photos sent in Rex chat are routed to the Receipt inbox (correct UX boundary).

## 5. Coverage claims vs. actual contractor availability — 1 FIX APPLIED, 1 FLAG

- **Correct by design:** intake is open nationwide; "Same-Day Availability" is only shown where the live coverage engine verifies capacity; request-status pages confirm availability after the request. `SameDayPickup` FAQ states same-day only where a verified partner is confirmed.
- **FIXED (this audit):** Rex's scripted claim that guaranteed same-day applied to 5 hardcoded operator cities contradicted the live engine. Updated to defer to live verified capacity.
- **FLAG (recommended next):** `nationalInquiry` marks all 29 `ACTIVE_CITIES` as `coverage_status: 'verified'` ("operator cities have guaranteed same-day capacity"), while the live verified capacity is currently **North Vancouver only** (1 active verified partner: 24H Junk). Customer-facing wording in that message stays honest ("dispatch is arranging your local contractor… before the appointment is guaranteed"), but the stored `verified` status can surface as stronger capacity language on admin surfaces. Recommend deriving `verified` from live Partner/coverage data, or renaming the status (e.g. `operator_city`) to decouple it from capacity claims.
